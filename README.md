# Email & SMS Retention Skills — by Ground

13 AI-powered retention workflows for DTC and ecommerce teams. Built for heads of lifecycle marketing, retention, and ecommerce.

## What's Inside

### Flow Architecture & Audit
- **Flow Audit & Gap Finder** — Audits your email/SMS flows against a 15-flow benchmark map and finds every gap
- **Flow Builder & Drafter** — Builds complete flows with triggers, timing, splits, and copy — Klaviyo-ready
- **Welcome Series Optimizer** — Splits your welcome by purchase status (the #1 fix from Klaviyo's 100-brand audit)
- **Abandonment Flow Suite** — Coordinated cart, browse, and checkout recovery that works together

### Segmentation & Personalization
- **Segmentation Auditor & Builder** — Audits against the 15+ segment standard, builds RFM, engagement, lifecycle, and suppression segments
- **Customer Journey Mapper** — Maps your customer journey and finds where automations should exist but don't
- **Personalization Engine** — Adds dynamic content blocks, product recs, and Klaviyo template code to every flow

### Performance & Optimization
- **Flow Performance Reviewer** — Monthly reviews benchmarked against Klaviyo 2026 data with specific fix recommendations
- **Deliverability & List Health Auditor** — Checks authentication, sender reputation, and Gmail/Yahoo 2024+ compliance
- **Winback & Churn Prevention Strategist** — 3-layer defense: pre-churn interventions, winback flows, sunset sequences

### Campaign Strategy
- **Campaign Calendar Planner** — Plans your monthly email/SMS calendar with BFCM strategy and frequency by segment
- **SMS Strategy & Compliance** — When to text vs email, 160-character copy, TCPA compliance
- **Retention Dashboard & Reporting** — Moves from vanity metrics to business outcomes with cohort analysis

## Built On Real Benchmarks

Every skill is grounded in data from Klaviyo's 2026 audit of 100+ brands, Yotpo benchmarks, Attentive, Omniconvert, and 15+ other industry sources.
